# Privacy Policy – Wi-Fi Security Guardian

Last updated: October 2026

**Short version: the app collects nothing and sends nothing to anyone.**

- **What the app does:** it checks devices on the local network you are connected to for risky services.
- **Data stored:** scan history, device names you choose, and trusted devices are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- **Data shared:** none. The app has no accounts, analytics, ads or servers. It only communicates with devices on your local network.
- **Location permission (Android):** Android requires it to show the Wi-Fi network name. The app never reads or stores your location. You can deny it; scanning still works.
- **Children:** the app is not directed at children.
